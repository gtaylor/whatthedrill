## How to run it

1. The fielder starts in a ready stance about 30 feet from the thrower or coach.
2. The coach throws or hits a high fly ball well over the fielder's head.
3. The fielder takes a quick look, picks the spot where it will come down, then turns their head and back to the ball and sprints to that spot.
4. After a few strides, the fielder snaps the head back around to find the ball and adjusts to make the catch.
5. Do 8 to 10 reps, mixing both sides.

## Coaching cues

- "Pick a spot and go."
- "Get there, then find it."
- "Beat the ball to the spot."

## Common mistakes

- Watching the ball the whole way and drifting slowly back.
- Turning the head the wrong way and losing the ball for too long.
- Arriving late and jumping or lunging instead of being there early.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use shorter, higher throws so there's plenty of time to find the ball again.
- **Harder:** use a fungo, hit longer balls, and make the fielder finish with a throw to a cutoff player.
