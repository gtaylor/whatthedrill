## How to run it

1. Split the team into two equal lines. One line starts at home plate. The other starts at second base.
2. On "go," the first runner in each line sprints around all four bases, the same direction a runner would go, and ends back at their own line.
3. The runner tags the next teammate's hand. Then that teammate takes off.
4. Runners must touch every base. Miss one and you go back and touch it.
5. The first team to have every player finish wins.
6. Play 2 or 3 rounds. Switch which line starts at home.

## Coaching cues

- "Hit the inside corner."
- "Curve before the base."
- "Run through the hand tag."

## Common mistakes

- Running straight at each base and making square, wide turns.
- Slowing down to touch the bag.
- Next runner leaving early before the tag.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** each runner only goes halfway, from home to second or second to home, before tagging.
- **Harder:** give each runner a baseball to carry and hand off, like a baton.
