Warm up your arm with easy throws first. This drill is about good footwork, not a max-effort throw every time.

## How to run it

1. Partners stand about 60 feet apart, or about 40 feet for younger players. Start with the ball in your glove.
2. Take a short step toward your partner with your glove-side foot.
3. Hop forward, bringing your throwing-side foot through and landing on it turned sideways. Your front shoulder and glove should point at your partner.
4. Stride your glove-side foot toward your partner and throw. Let your back leg swing through after the throw.
5. Make 8 to 10 throws each. Walk through it slowly first, then speed it up.

## Coaching cues

- "Hop forward, not up."
- "Land sideways."
- "Shoulder points, then throw."

## Common mistakes

- Hopping high in the air instead of moving toward the target.
- Landing with the back foot pointed at the target, so the chest opens too early.
- Backing up too far and throwing with all-out effort, so the throws spray.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do the footwork with no ball, then throw from 30 feet.
- **Harder:** have your partner roll you a ball. Field it moving forward, then crow hop and throw.
