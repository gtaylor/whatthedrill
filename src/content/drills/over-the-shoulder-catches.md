## How to run it

1. Set a cone where the fielder starts. Set a second cone 25 to 30 yards straight ahead. The thrower stands next to the first cone.
2. The fielder drop-steps as if the ball went over their head, then runs toward the far cone.
3. When the fielder is about halfway, the thrower lobs the ball out ahead of them, over the glove-side shoulder.
4. The fielder keeps running hard, looks back over that shoulder, and reaches the glove up late to make the catch.
5. Do 5 over each shoulder. For the throwing-hand shoulder, the fielder catches with the glove reaching across.

## Coaching cues

- "Run on your toes so your eyes stay steady."
- "Glove up late."
- "Look over the shoulder, not around your body."

## Common mistakes

- Slowing down to look for the ball and letting it land in front.
- Running with the glove out, which slows you down.
- Turning the whole body around to face the thrower.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** jog instead of sprint, and use shorter, higher throws.
- **Harder:** throw it farther ahead so the fielder has to sprint to get there, or switch shoulders mid-run.
