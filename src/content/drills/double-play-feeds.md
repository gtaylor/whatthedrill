## How to run it

1. Put a shortstop and a second baseman at double-play depth, a little closer to second than usual, and a player at first. Extra players wait in line.
2. The coach stands near home and rolls or fungoes a ground ball to the shortstop.
3. The second baseman sprints to the bag, gets there early, and shows the glove as a chest-high target.
4. The shortstop feeds the ball. If close to the bag, use a firm underhand toss. If farther away, make a quick, firm throw.
5. The second baseman catches with two hands, touches the bag, steps toward first and throws. Then hit the next ball to second base so the shortstop turns it. Rotate after 5 of each.

## Coaching cues

- "Get to the bag early."
- "Feed it chest high."
- "Catch it first, then turn it."

## Common mistakes

- Rushing the throw to first before the ball is caught.
- Feeding with a soft lob that makes the pivot player wait.
- Arriving at the bag late and catching the feed while running across it.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start with no throw to first. Just practice clean feeds and catching at the bag.
- **Harder:** hit balls to both sides of the fielders, and time each turn from the hit to the catch at first.
