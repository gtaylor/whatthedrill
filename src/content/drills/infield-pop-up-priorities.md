Start with lower pop-ups and walk through the calls before going full speed. Collisions are what this drill prevents.

## How to run it

1. Put players at all six infield spots. Go over who has priority:
   - Shortstop and second base take anything they can reach.
   - First and third base come next, then the catcher.
   - The pitcher calls out who should take it but doesn't catch it.
2. The coach hits or throws a pop-up between two or three fielders.
3. Whoever has it calls "Ball! Ball! Ball!" loudly with an arm up. Everyone else peels off and backs them up.
4. If two players call it, the one with priority keeps it. The other one says "Take it!" and moves out of the way.
5. Do 15 to 20 pop-ups, moving them around the infield and near the foul lines and plate.

## Coaching cues

- "Call it loud, call it three times."
- "Hear a call, get out."
- "Back up the catch."

## Common mistakes

- Calling for it too late, after both players are already running full speed.
- Calling it quietly, or only once.
- Everyone stopping to watch instead of backing up the player making the catch.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use tennis balls and throw lower pop-ups, and walk through each spot first.
- **Harder:** add outfielders, who have priority over infielders on balls they can reach coming in, and hit pop-ups into the shallow outfield.
