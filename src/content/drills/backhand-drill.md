## How to run it

1. The fielder kneels with the partner about 10 feet away. The partner rolls balls to the fielder's backhand side (the throwing-hand side).
2. The fielder turns the glove over so the pocket faces the ball and the thumb points down, then catches it out in front of the body.
3. After 10 good reps on the knees, stand up. The partner rolls balls a step or two to the backhand side.
4. For a right-handed fielder, step across with the left foot, stay low and catch the ball out in front of that left foot. A lefty does the same with the right foot.
5. Finish each catch by planting the back foot and setting up to throw. Do 10, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "Turn it over and show the pocket."
- "Catch it in front of your foot."
- "Stay low through the catch."

## Common mistakes

- Reaching back and catching the ball beside or behind the body.
- Standing up while moving to the ball, so it rolls under the glove.
- Using the backhand on balls you could have moved your feet to get in front of.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** stay on the knees longer and use tennis balls.
- **Harder:** roll or hit balls farther to the side and harder, and add a full throw to first base.
