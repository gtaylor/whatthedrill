The tosser stays behind the L-screen for every toss.

## How to run it

1. The partner gives front toss from behind an L-screen. Start the ladder on the bottom rung.
2. Rung 1: hit a hard ground ball.
3. Rung 2: hit a line drive. Rung 3: hit a high line drive or fly ball.
4. Hit each rung once in order. If you miss a rung, start that rung again.
5. Climb up the ladder, then back down. Each player gets 3 full ladders.

## Coaching cues

- "Same hard swing every time."
- "Hit the bottom half to lift it."
- "Know your rung before the toss."

## Common mistakes

- Swinging softer to hit the ground ball, then harder for the fly ball.
- Dropping the back shoulder way down to lift the ball.
- Guessing the rung after the swing instead of planning it before.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** hit off a tee, and use only two rungs: ground ball and line drive.
- **Harder:** the partner calls a random rung right as the toss leaves the hand.
