## How to run it

1. Partners stand 15 to 20 feet apart in a ready position, knees bent and hands out in front. Go closer for younger players.
2. Catch the ball out in front of your body with two hands, throwing hand right next to the glove.
3. As the ball hits the glove, bring glove and hand together up toward your throwing shoulder and grab the ball.
4. Throw it right back to your partner's chest with a short, quick arm swing. Keep your feet moving with small steps.
5. Go for 30 seconds and count catches in a row with no drop or bad throw. Rest, then do 3 or 4 rounds.

## Coaching cues

- "Two hands, quick hands."
- "Glove to your throwing shoulder."
- "Get it, grip it, go."

## Common mistakes

- Catching off to the side with one hand, so the throwing hand has to chase the ball.
- Looking down into the glove to find the ball.
- Throwing harder instead of moving the hands faster, which leads to wild throws.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start with soft underhand tosses and a slow pace, then speed up.
- **Harder:** try to beat your best count, and find a four-seam grip on every throw.
