Use a small hand towel and make sure nobody is standing close in front of you.

## How to run it

1. Hold one end of a hand towel between your pointer and middle fingers, like your fastball grip. Let 6 to 8 inches hang out.
2. Set a target. Take your normal stride and mark where your front foot lands. A partner kneels or stands about one big step past that spot and holds a glove out at chest height. If you're alone, set a glove on a bucket or chair at the same spot.
3. Go through your full pitching motion and snap the towel at the glove.
4. Finish all the way. Your throwing shoulder should end up over your front knee and your back foot should come off the ground.
5. Do 10 to 15 reps. If you hit the glove every time, move it a few inches farther away.

## Coaching cues

- "Reach out to the glove."
- "Chest over your front knee."
- "Finish like a real pitch."

## Common mistakes

- Bending at the waist and lunging to reach the glove instead of using the legs.
- Stopping the arm as soon as the towel snaps, with no follow-through.
- Changing the stride length on every rep, so the target is never in the same place.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** skip the target and just work on a smooth, full finish.
- **Harder:** do 5 towel reps, then throw 5 real pitches to a catcher with the same finish.
