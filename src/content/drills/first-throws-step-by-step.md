## How to run it

1. Stand 15 to 20 feet apart. Show the grip: two fingers on top of the ball, thumb underneath.
2. **Turn.** The player turns sideways so the glove shoulder points at the partner.
3. **Point.** The glove arm points at the target while the throwing hand reaches back, with the ball facing away from the target.
4. **Step.** The player steps toward the target with the glove-side foot.
5. **Throw.** Bring the arm over the top and let go out in front. Finish with the throwing hand down by the opposite knee.
6. Say each word out loud for the first 10 throws. Then put it together into one smooth motion.

## Coaching cues

- "Turn, point, step, throw."
- "Point your glove at your target."
- "Finish down by your other knee."

## Common mistakes

- Facing the target square and pushing the ball like a dart.
- Stepping with the same foot as the throwing arm.
- Letting go too early, so the ball goes straight up.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw at a big target like a fence or a hanging towel from 10 feet, no catcher needed.
- **Harder:** back up 5 feet after every 5 good throws and count how many in a row reach the partner's chest.
