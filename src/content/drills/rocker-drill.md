Warm up fully first, count these throws as part of the day's throwing, and stop if anything hurts.

## How to run it

1. Partners stand 45 to 60 feet apart. The pitcher stands sideways, feet a little wider than the shoulders, front foot pointed at the partner.
2. Hold the ball in the glove at the chest. Rock your weight onto the back leg, then onto the front leg, then back again. Do two or three easy rocks to feel the rhythm.
3. On the last rock back, break the hands apart and push forward off the back leg into the throw.
4. Let the hips lead and the arm follow. Finish with your chest over the front leg and the back leg coming through.
5. Make 10 to 12 throws at about three-quarter effort. Throw a little firmer only if the throws stay on target.

## Coaching cues

- "Rock, rock, go."
- "Push off the back leg."
- "Hips first, then the arm."

## Common mistakes

- Starting the arm before the weight moves forward, so it's still all arm.
- Leaning the upper body back instead of shifting the weight onto the back leg.
- Going all-out on every throw and losing control of where the ball goes.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** shorten the distance and throw at half speed until the timing feels smooth.
- **Harder:** add a step-behind with the back foot before the last rock, then throw to a catcher.
