Many leagues for 12 and under don't allow leading off. Check your league's rules first.

## How to run it

1. Stand on the base with your left foot touching it, facing home plate.
2. Take your lead: step off with your right foot, then shuffle sideways toward second without crossing your feet. Stop at the same spot every time, about one big step plus your body length off the bag.
3. Get into your stance: feet a little wider than your shoulders, knees bent, weight on the balls of your feet, hands loose in front.
4. Say "back!" to yourself. Cross your right foot over toward the base and get back to the bag fast, touching it with your hand or foot.
5. Do 10 leads and returns. Then do 5 where you shuffle twice more as if the pitch were thrown (a secondary lead), stop, and get back.

## Coaching cues

- "Same lead every time."
- "Shuffle, don't cross."
- "Weight on the balls of your feet."

## Common mistakes

- Taking a different size lead every time, which tips off the pitcher.
- Crossing your feet while leading off, which leaves you stuck if there's a pickoff.
- Standing tall and flat-footed, which makes you slow both ways.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** mark your lead spot with a glove or a line in the dirt.
- **Harder:** a partner plays pitcher and mixes in pickoff moves, and you only learn which way to go at the last second.
