## How to run it

1. Put a base or a line on the grass. Put a cone 15 to 20 yards away, the way you would run to second.
2. Get in a lead stance next to the base: feet a little wider than the shoulders, knees bent, weight on the balls of the feet.
3. On your own "go," turn your right foot open toward the cone. At the same time, drive your left leg across your body. That's the crossover.
4. Stay low and pump your arms hard. Your first few steps should push the ground behind you, not pop you up tall.
5. Sprint all the way past the cone. Walk back and rest a few breaths before the next one.
6. Do 6 to 8 starts.

## Coaching cues

- "Open and cross."
- "Stay low out of it."
- "Arms go first."

## Common mistakes

- Standing straight up on the first step and losing power.
- Stepping backward with the right foot instead of opening it toward second.
- Slowing down after a few steps instead of sprinting through the cone.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** walk through the crossover step slowly 5 times before doing it at full speed.
- **Harder:** have a partner act as the pitcher and go only when they lift their front leg, or run against a stopwatch.
