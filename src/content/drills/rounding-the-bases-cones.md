## How to run it

1. Set a base. Put the first cone 15 feet before it and 3 or 4 feet off the line to the outside. Put the second cone 15 feet past the base toward the next base.
2. Start 30 feet before the first cone and sprint toward it.
3. Curve out around the first cone so you're heading straight at the base, not across it.
4. Touch the inside corner of the base with either foot without slowing down. Lean your inside shoulder in as you turn.
5. Run hard to the second cone, then stop and walk back. Do 8 turns, resting between each one.

## Coaching cues

- "Curve early."
- "Inside corner."
- "Lean in, keep your speed."

## Common mistakes

- Running straight at the base and making a big loop after it.
- Chopping your steps to hit the bag with one certain foot.
- Standing straight up through the turn instead of leaning in.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** walk, then jog, the path first, and add a third cone to mark the curve.
- **Harder:** set two bases 60 feet apart (shorter for younger players) and round both in one run, like a double.
