Jog and do a quick moving warm-up first. Anyone whose arm hurts stops throwing and tells a coach.

## How to run it

1. Pair up and make two long lines facing each other, about 15 feet apart. Everyone throws in the same direction so no one is in a throwing lane.
2. Wrist flicks: elbow up, flick the ball with just the wrist. 10 each.
3. One knee: throwing-side knee down, 20 to 30 feet apart. 10 each.
4. Standing sideways with the glove shoulder at your partner, feet set, turn and throw. 10 each at 30 to 40 feet.
5. Full throws with a step, then a crow hop, backing up to about 60 to 90 feet depending on age. Finish with a minute of quick, short throws back up close.

## Coaching cues

- "Every throw has a target."
- "Same grip every time."
- "Back up only when throws are on line."

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the short steps and going straight to long, hard throws.
- Lazy throws with no target, which build bad habits.
- Players wandering into the next pair's throwing lane.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** for young players, cut it to three steps and keep the distances short.
- **Harder:** end with a timed quick-hands round and see which pair gets the most clean catches.
