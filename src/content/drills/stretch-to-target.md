Count every pitch, and follow pitch-count and rest guidelines such as MLB and USA Baseball's Pitch Smart.

## How to run it

1. The catcher sets up with full gear and gives a target. The pitcher stands sideways with the back foot along the front edge of the rubber and the front foot a little toward the plate.
2. Look in at the catcher with the hands apart. Then bring the hands together at the belt and come to a full stop for a second.
3. Use a quick, short leg lift, quicker than in the windup, and throw to the glove.
4. The catcher moves the target inside or outside and calls it out before each pitch. Count how many pitches land on the called side.
5. Throw 15 to 25 pitches. Change how long you stay set, one second or two, but always come to a full stop.

## Coaching cues

- "Come to a full stop."
- "Eyes on the glove."
- "Quick lift, same finish."

## Common mistakes

- Not stopping in the set position, which is a balk with runners on base.
- Rushing the body so the arm falls behind and pitches sail high.
- Holding the set the same length every time, so runners can time it.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw from a shorter distance and keep the target in the middle.
- **Harder:** put a coach at first base as a runner. Look over, hold, then deliver a strike.
