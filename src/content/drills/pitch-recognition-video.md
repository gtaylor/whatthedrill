## How to run it

1. Find pitching videos filmed from behind the catcher or from center field, so you see what a hitter sees.
2. Stand in your stance in front of the screen. Start your load as the pitcher starts the delivery.
3. On each pitch, call "yes" (strike, I'm swinging) or "no" (ball, I'm taking it) before it reaches the plate. Play it out to check.
4. Next round: pause the video right after the ball leaves the pitcher's hand. Call "fastball" or "off-speed," then play it to check.
5. Do 20 pitches and keep score. Try to beat your score next time.

## Coaching cues

- "Find the release point."
- "Decide early: yes or no."
- "Pick up the spin."

## Common mistakes

- Watching the pitcher's face or glove instead of the spot where the ball comes out.
- Waiting until the ball is on top of you to decide.
- Doing it too long. Ten focused minutes beats an hour of half-watching.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** only call strike or ball, and use slow-motion clips.
- **Harder:** call the pitch type and the location ("fastball, in"), or stop the video even earlier.
