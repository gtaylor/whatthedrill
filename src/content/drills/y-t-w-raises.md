No weights are needed. Stop if anything pinches or hurts.

## How to run it

1. Bend forward at the hips with a flat back and soft knees, arms hanging down. You can also lie face down on a mat.
2. Y: thumbs up, raise both arms forward and out into a Y shape, up to about ear height. Hold for 2 seconds, then lower slowly. 10 reps.
3. T: thumbs up, raise the arms straight out to the sides into a T and squeeze the shoulder blades. 10 reps.
4. W: bend the elbows and pull them back and down toward your hips, making a W. Squeeze, then relax. 10 reps.
5. Rest for a minute and do one more round.

## Coaching cues

- "Thumbs to the sky."
- "Squeeze the shoulder blades together."
- "Slow up, slow down."

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging the shoulders up toward the ears.
- Swinging the arms up fast instead of lifting under control.
- Arching the lower back to get the arms higher.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** lie face down on the floor and make smaller lifts.
- **Harder:** hold each shape for 5 seconds, or hold a light resistance band between your hands.
