Every hitter wears a helmet at the toss and cage stations. Keep waiting players well outside swinging range.

## How to run it

1. Split the team into four groups of 2 to 4 players. Put one adult or older player at each station.
2. Station 1 is tee work into a net. Use the inside and outside spots.
3. Station 2 is soft toss into a net. Station 3 is the fence drill or dry swings in front of a fence.
4. Station 4 is the cage, with front toss from behind an L-screen.
5. Give each station 7 to 8 minutes. Blow a whistle and rotate until every group has hit at every station.

## Coaching cues

- "Every swing has a target."
- "Short and quick to the ball."
- "Work fast. Get your reps."

## Common mistakes

- Groups that are too big, so players wait in line instead of swinging.
- Stations set up too close together, so swings and balls cross paths.
- Rushing through swings with no goal just to get through the bucket.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use three stations and give younger players foam or plastic balls at the toss stations.
- **Harder:** keep score at each station, like points for line drives into the back of the net.
