The pitcher throws from behind an L-screen. Fielders wear gloves and stay alert when a ball is hit.

## How to run it

1. Split the team into two teams. One team hits while the other plays the field and shags.
2. A coach pitches from behind an L-screen. Use cones to mark a line in the outfield grass just past the infield dirt.
3. Each hitter gets 6 pitches. A line drive that lands past the cones scores 2 points. A hard ground ball through the infield scores 1.
4. Pop-ups, weak rollers and misses score 0. A ball caught in the air by a fielder also scores 0.
5. After everyone hits, switch sides. Play 2 or 3 rounds and add up the points.

## Coaching cues

- "Hit it hard and on a line."
- "Through the middle of the ball."
- "Don't try to hit a home run."

## Common mistakes

- Uppercutting to hit home runs, which leads to pop-ups.
- Fielders standing still and not hustling after balls.
- Hitters waiting in line too close to the batter.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** hit off a tee or coach toss for younger players, and give 1 point for any ball hit in the air.
- **Harder:** use a pitching machine at game speed, or only count line drives to the gaps.
