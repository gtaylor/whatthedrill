## How to run it

1. Partners kneel 10 feet apart. The fielder has no glove. Roll a soft ball right at them, and they scoop it up with two hands.
2. Stand up. The fielder gets in a ready position: feet wide, knees bent, butt down, hands out in front.
3. Roll more soft balls, then bounce a few. The fielder keeps the eyes on the ball and lets it come into the hands.
4. Put the glove on and repeat with a tennis ball. Keep the throwing hand right above the glove.
5. Only when the player asks for it, move to a real ball rolled slowly. Go back to the soft ball any time they flinch.

## Coaching cues

- "Watch it all the way in."
- "Nose over the ball, hands out front."
- "Soft hands, like catching an egg."

## Common mistakes

- Turning the head away or closing the eyes as the ball arrives.
- Standing up tall and reaching down at the last second.
- Rushing to a real, hard ball before the player is ready.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** sit on the ground facing each other and roll the ball back and forth.
- **Harder:** move back, add short hops with the tennis ball, and roll some balls a step to each side.
