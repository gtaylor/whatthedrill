## How to run it

1. Stand 6 to 10 feet apart. The player puts the glove on and holds both hands out in front, elbows a little bent.
2. Toss a tennis ball underhand at the player's chest. Before they catch it, they show "thumbs": glove fingers up, thumbs touching.
3. Next, toss at the knees. Now they show "pinkies": fingers point down, pinkies touching, like a basket.
4. Mix the tosses. Call out "thumbs" or "pinkies" only after the ball leaves your hand, so the player has to decide.
5. Catch with two hands every time. The throwing hand closes over the ball in the glove.
6. Do 20 to 30 catches, then take one small step back.

## Coaching cues

- "Thumbs up high, pinkies down low."
- "Watch it all the way into the glove."
- "Two hands, every time."

## Common mistakes

- Holding the glove flat, like a plate, so the ball bounces out.
- Closing the eyes or turning the head away as the ball arrives.
- Catching next to the body instead of out in front.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start bare-handed with a big soft ball, then add the glove.
- **Harder:** toss a little to the left and right, and switch to a real ball once 10 catches in a row feel easy.
