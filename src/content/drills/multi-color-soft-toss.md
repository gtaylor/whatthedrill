The tosser kneels to the side and a little in front of the hitter, never in front of the swing. Use soft or plastic balls when you toss two at once.

## How to run it

1. The hitter faces a net or fence. The tosser kneels off to the side, about 6 feet away.
2. Start with one ball at a time. The hitter calls out the color before swinging, then hits it into the net.
3. Toss to the front hip so the hitter can drive the ball straight ahead.
4. Next, hold two different colors in one hand. Toss both and call one color as they leave your hand.
5. The hitter hits only the called color. Do 3 rounds of 10, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "See the color, then swing."
- "Head still. Eyes on the ball."
- "Let it come to you."

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the head out early to see where the ball went.
- The tosser throwing at the hitter's body instead of out in front.
- Swinging at both balls instead of waiting for the call.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use one ball and one color. Just call the color out loud before every swing.
- **Harder:** use three colors, call the color later, or mark numbers on the balls instead of colors.
