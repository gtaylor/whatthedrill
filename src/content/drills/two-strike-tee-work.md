## How to run it

1. Set the tee on the outer half of the plate, where pitchers like to throw with two strikes.
2. Take 5 normal swings to feel your regular swing.
3. Now switch to your two-strike setup: choke up about an inch, widen your stance a little, and use a smaller stride.
4. Take 5 two-strike swings. Aim to drive the ball up the middle or the other way, not pull it.
5. Move the tee around the edges of the zone (low, away, in) and keep going. Do 4 rounds.

## Coaching cues

- "Choke and widen."
- "Up the middle."
- "Protect the plate. Anything close, you swing."

## Common mistakes

- Turning the two-strike swing into a slow, weak slap. It's shorter, not softer.
- Keeping the big stride, which makes timing harder.
- Pulling off the ball on the outside pitch.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** practice the setup with dry swings in front of a mirror before using the tee.
- **Harder:** have a partner do front toss and shout "two strikes!" at random, so you switch setups on the fly.
