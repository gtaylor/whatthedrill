For players 13 and up. Swing in a wide-open space with nobody nearby, warm up first, and stop when your swing starts to break down.

## How to run it

1. Warm up with easy throws and dry swings with your game bat.
2. Use a heavy bat only a little heavier than your game bat, and a light bat only a little lighter. Big jumps in weight change your swing.
3. Take 5 full-speed swings with the heavy bat. Rest about 30 seconds.
4. Take 5 swings with the light bat, then 5 with your game bat. Swing as fast as you can every time.
5. That's one round. Do 3 rounds. Hit off a tee, or do them as dry swings.

## Coaching cues

- "Every swing at max speed."
- "Same swing with every bat."
- "Rest. Fresh swings are fast swings."

## Common mistakes

- Using a bat so heavy that the swing gets long and slow.
- Taking too many swings, so the last ones are tired and sloppy.
- Swinging a weighted donut on deck. It can mess up your timing and doesn't help bat speed.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use only your game bat and a light bat for the first couple of weeks.
- **Harder:** measure bat speed with a sensor or radar and try to beat your best each session.
