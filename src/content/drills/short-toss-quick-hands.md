The tosser must stay fully behind a sturdy L-screen and pull the arm back behind it after every toss.

## How to run it

1. The tosser sits or kneels behind an L-screen, closer than normal front toss, about 12 to 15 feet away.
2. Toss firm, quick pitches through the middle of the zone.
3. The hitter starts the load early, as the tosser's arm comes forward.
4. Swing short and quick. Drive the ball back up the middle.
5. Do 3 rounds of 10 at a fast pace, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "Start early. Be on time."
- "Hands go straight to the ball."
- "Short to it, long through it."

## Common mistakes

- Starting the load late and getting jammed.
- Making a long, loopy swing that can't catch up.
- The tosser leaning out from behind the screen to watch.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** back up a few feet and toss a little softer.
- **Harder:** move a little closer or toss faster, and hit 5 balls in a row without a miss.
