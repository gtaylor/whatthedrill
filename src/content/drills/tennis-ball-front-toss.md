## How to run it

1. The tosser kneels or sits 12 to 15 feet in front of the hitter, behind a screen if you have one. The hitter wears a helmet.
2. Before any swings, toss a few tennis balls gently at the hitter's leg or back so they feel how little it hurts. Only do this if the player says it's okay.
3. Toss underhand strikes. The hitter's job is to step toward the tosser, keep the front shoulder pointed at them, and swing.
4. After each swing, check the front foot. It should land pointing at the tosser, not off toward the dugout side.
5. Do 3 rounds of 10 swings. Praise every brave step, even on a miss.

## Coaching cues

- "Step to me."
- "Front shoulder stays pointed at me."
- "It's just a tennis ball. You've got this."

## Common mistakes

- Stepping away from the plate, which makes the outside pitch impossible to reach.
- Turning the head or closing the eyes as the ball comes in.
- Tossing too hard too soon. Speed comes only after the player is relaxed.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** move closer and toss slower, or start with a big foam ball.
- **Harder:** move back to 20 feet, toss a little firmer, and switch to wiffle balls before real ones.
