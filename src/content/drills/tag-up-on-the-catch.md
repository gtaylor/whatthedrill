## How to run it

1. The runner stands on third base with a helmet on. The partner stands in shallow left field, 40 to 60 feet away, with a glove and ball.
2. The runner puts one foot on the base and turns so they can see the partner and still push off toward home.
3. The partner throws the ball high into the air to themselves and catches it.
4. The runner takes off the instant the ball touches the glove and sprints hard toward home for 5 or 6 strides.
5. Walk back and switch roles after 8 to 10 tags. Leaving before the catch counts as an out.

## Coaching cues

- "Go on the catch."
- "Watch the glove, not the ball in the air."
- "Explode off the bag."

## Common mistakes

- Leaving early because you guessed the ball would be caught. That's an out in a game.
- Standing flat with both feet on the base, so the first step is slow.
- Watching the ball until it lands instead of running the moment it's caught.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** the partner calls "now!" as the ball is caught until the runner gets the timing.
- **Harder:** the partner sometimes drops the ball or bobbles it. The runner has to stay on the bag until the catch is clean.
