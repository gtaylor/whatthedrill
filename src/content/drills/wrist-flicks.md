## How to run it

1. Partners stand about 10 feet apart, facing each other, feet about shoulder-width.
2. Hold the ball with a four-seam grip. Pointer and middle fingers go across the wide seam, and the thumb goes underneath.
3. Lift the throwing elbow to shoulder height out in front of you, with the hand above the elbow. Put your glove hand under the elbow to hold it still.
4. Tip your wrist back, then flick it forward so the ball rolls off your first two fingers to your partner's chest.
5. Watch the spin. A good flick spins the seams straight back, top to bottom. Make 10 to 15 flicks each.

## Coaching cues

- "Fingers on top of the ball."
- "Hold the elbow still."
- "Pull down through the seams."

## Common mistakes

- Moving the elbow or shoulder, which turns the flick into a push.
- Holding the ball deep in the palm instead of in the fingers.
- Letting the fingers slide to the side of the ball, which makes it spin sideways and tail.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** stand closer and use a tennis ball or soft ball.
- **Harder:** back up to 15 feet. Your partner only counts the flick if the seams spin straight back.
