## How to run it

1. Set two markers a little wider than shoulder width for the feet. Put a third marker about an arm's length in front, halfway between them. That is the triangle.
2. The fielder stands with a foot on each back marker, knees bent and butt down, and the glove open on the front marker.
3. The partner rolls the ball from about 20 feet into the point of the triangle. The fielder catches it there, out in front, not between the feet.
4. After the catch, bring the ball to the belly. A right-handed fielder steps the right foot toward the target, then the left, and throws back. A lefty does the opposite.
5. Do 10 reps, then switch. Take the markers away and see if the fielder can still find the triangle.

## Coaching cues

- "Catch it at the point."
- "Butt down, back flat."
- "Right, left, throw."

## Common mistakes

- Letting the ball get between the feet instead of catching it out front.
- Bending at the waist with straight legs.
- Throwing from flat feet without stepping toward the target.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** skip the throw and just catch at the point and bring it to the belly.
- **Harder:** roll the ball to one side so the fielder has to move and rebuild the triangle before catching it.
