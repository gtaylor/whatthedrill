Wear full catcher's gear. Start with soft balls until the block is solid.

## How to run it

1. The catcher sets up behind the plate in the secondary stance. A partner throws balls into the dirt from about 30 feet. A third player (or the thrower) acts as the pitcher covering home.
2. The catcher blocks the ball, keeping it in front.
3. Right away, pop up and find the ball. Get to it with quick steps and turn the shoulders toward home.
4. Pick it up with the bare hand and make a firm, short flip or throw to the pitcher, who has run in to cover home plate.
5. Do 8 to 10 reps. Then have the partner roll a few balls past the catcher to the backstop, and practice turning, running to it and throwing back to the plate.

## Coaching cues

- "Block first, then pounce."
- "Shoulders to home before you throw."
- "Short, firm throw."

## Common mistakes

- Starting to get up before the block is finished, so the ball gets past.
- Taking a big, slow windup on a 20-foot throw.
- The pitcher watching the ball instead of sprinting to cover home.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** skip the throw at first. Just block, pounce and hold the ball up.
- **Harder:** add a runner on third who breaks for home on every ball in the dirt, and keep score of runs saved.
