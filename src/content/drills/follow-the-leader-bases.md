## How to run it

1. Line the players up behind home plate in helmets. The coach stands at the front of the line.
2. Jog around the bases with the players following in a single line. At each bag, everyone touches it and shouts its name: "First!", "Second!", "Third!", "Home!"
3. Go around two or three times. Then the coach stops at the pitcher's mound and points while the players run the loop on their own.
4. Now quiz them. Put one player on a base and ask, "Where do you run next?" They point, then run there.
5. Finish with each player running all four bases alone while the team counts the bases out loud.

## Coaching cues

- "First, second, third, home."
- "Touch every bag with your foot."
- "Run on the dirt path, not across the grass."

## Common mistakes

- Running from home toward third base instead of first.
- Skipping a base or cutting across the infield to get home faster.
- Stopping between bases to look at the coach. Keep running and listen.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** put a parent or coach at every base to hold up a hand for a high five.
- **Harder:** call out a base, like "second," and have the runner start from home and stop on the right one.
