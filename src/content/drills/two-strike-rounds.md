The coach pitches or feeds the machine from behind an L-screen. Hitters wait well outside swinging range.

## How to run it

1. Each hitter steps in with a count of 1-2. A coach calls balls and strikes.
2. Before the first pitch, the hitter makes a two-strike change: choke up an inch, spread the feet a little wider and shorten the stride.
3. The coach mixes strikes with pitches just off the plate.
4. Put the ball in play or draw a walk and you score 1 point. A strikeout scores 0. Foul balls keep you alive.
5. Give each hitter 3 at-bats, then rotate. Keep a team score.

## Coaching cues

- "Choke up and battle."
- "Protect the plate. Foul off the tough ones."
- "Hit it up the middle."

## Common mistakes

- Swinging just as big as you do with no strikes.
- Taking a called strike three on a pitch that was close.
- Chasing pitches in the dirt because you're scared to strike out.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start each at-bat at 2-2, and throw mostly strikes.
- **Harder:** start at 0-2, use a faster machine speed, or mix in off-speed pitches.
