## How to run it

1. Put runners at first, second and third with helmets on. A catcher in gear sets up behind home. A coach kneels or sits on a bucket in front of the mound.
2. The coach throws soft pitches to the catcher. Most are normal. Some are aimed to bounce in front of the plate.
3. On every pitch, runners take their secondary lead and watch the ball, not the catcher.
4. When the ball is headed down into the dirt, the runner yells "Dirt!" and sprints to the next base.
5. When the pitch is caught clean, the runner stops, turns, and shuffles back to the base.
6. After each rep, runners rotate one base. Runners from third go to the back of the line at first.

## Coaching cues

- "Watch the ball, not the catcher."
- "See it go down, then go."
- "Trust your read."

## Common mistakes

- Waiting to see if the catcher blocks it, which costs the jump.
- Going on every low pitch, even ones that will be caught.
- Taking a lazy secondary lead and having too far to run.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** the coach rolls or bounces the ball by hand from a few feet in front of the plate so the read is obvious.
- **Harder:** the catcher tries to block and throw out the runner, so runners learn which dirt balls are worth taking.
