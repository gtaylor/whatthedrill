Keep a pitch count for each pitcher and follow pitch-count and rest guidelines such as MLB and USA Baseball's Pitch Smart.

## How to run it

1. Set up a strike zone target, or a catcher in full gear, at the right pitching distance for the players' age.
2. Pitchers line up. Each one faces an imaginary batter and throws until they get 3 strikes (a strikeout) or 4 balls (a walk).
3. A strikeout is worth 1 point. A walk takes away 1 point. Each pitcher faces 3 batters, then the next pitcher goes.
4. A coach keeps score and counts every pitch. Each pitcher stops at a set number, such as 25 to 30 pitches.
5. After everyone has had two turns, the highest score wins.

## Coaching cues

- "Get ahead with strike one."
- "Pick a small target."
- "Same motion every pitch."

## Common mistakes

- Aiming the ball and slowing the arm down, which makes it harder to throw strikes.
- Getting upset after a ball and rushing the next pitch.
- Throwing too many pitches because nobody is counting.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use a bigger target or a shorter distance.
- **Harder:** the catcher calls inside or outside before each pitch, and only strikes on that half count.
