## How to run it

1. Set the tee for an **inside pitch**: out in front of your front foot, over the inside edge of the plate.
2. Take 5 swings. Hit hard line drives toward the pull-side gap.
3. Move the tee for an **outside pitch**: deeper (even with the middle of your stance) and over the outside edge.
4. Take 5 swings. Let the ball travel and drive it to the opposite field.
5. Keep alternating sets of 5 until the bucket is empty.

## Coaching cues

- "Inside: catch it out front."
- "Outside: let it get deep."
- "Hands stay inside the ball."

## Common mistakes

- Hitting both locations at the same spot instead of moving the contact point.
- Reaching for the outside ball with the arms, which makes the swing long.
- Rolling the top hand over at contact, which turns line drives into weak grounders.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start with the tee in the middle of the plate, then add the inside spot.
- **Harder:** set up two tees and have a partner call "in" or "out" as you start your swing.
