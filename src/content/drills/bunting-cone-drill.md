Keep the fingers of your top hand behind the bat, never wrapped around the front. The tosser throws from behind an L-screen.

## How to run it

1. Use cones to make a box about 3 steps wide down the first base line, about 15 to 20 feet from home. Make a second box down the third base line.
2. A partner throws soft pitches from behind an L-screen, about 30 feet away.
3. Square to bunt. Slide the top hand up the bat, pinch it with the thumb and first finger, and keep your fingers behind the bat.
4. Hold the bat at the top of the zone, with the end a little higher than your hands. Bend your knees for low pitches.
5. Catch the ball with the bat and aim for a box. Score 1 point per bunt in a box. Take 10 bunts, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "Catch it with the bat."
- "Fingers behind the bat."
- "Bend your knees, not the bat."

## Common mistakes

- Wrapping the top-hand fingers around the bat, where the ball can hit them.
- Jabbing at the ball instead of letting it hit the bat.
- Dropping the barrel below the hands to reach a low pitch.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** make the boxes bigger and toss slower, or use a soft ball.
- **Harder:** make the boxes smaller and call "first" or "third" as the pitch is thrown.
