Start with a tennis ball until you're catching it cleanly every time.

## How to run it

1. Stand in an open space with your glove on. Throw the ball straight up, as high as you can control it.
2. Get your feet under the ball and catch it above your throwing-side shoulder, with your throwing hand right next to the glove.
3. Do 10 in a row. Count how many you catch without a drop.
4. Now throw it up and a little in front of you, so you have to take a few steps in. Then a little behind you, so you have to drop step and go back.
5. Finish with 5 throws where you catch it and step into a throw at a target, like you would in a game.

## Coaching cues

- "Feet first, then glove."
- "Catch it above your throwing shoulder."
- "Two hands."

## Common mistakes

- Standing still and reaching for the ball instead of moving your feet to get under it.
- Catching the ball down by the belt, where it's easy to drop.
- Running with the glove up the whole way, which slows you down. Run first, then put the glove up.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw lower and use a tennis ball or a soft ball.
- **Harder:** throw higher, turn your back for a second before looking up to find it, or catch it on the run.
