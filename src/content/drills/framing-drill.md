Wear full catcher's gear: helmet with mask, throat guard, chest protector, shin guards and a cup.

## How to run it

1. The catcher sets up behind the plate in the primary stance. The partner throws from about 35 to 40 feet.
2. Start with pitches down the middle. Catch the ball with a relaxed glove and hold it still for one second.
3. Move to the corners. On a pitch on the outside edge, catch the outer half of the ball so the glove doesn't drift out of the zone.
4. On a low pitch, get the glove under the ball and catch it with the fingers pointing up or sideways, so it doesn't get pushed down.
5. Throw 10 to each edge: inside, outside, low and high. Then mix them up.

## Coaching cues

- "Soft hand, quiet glove."
- "Catch it and stick it."
- "Beat the ball to the spot."

## Common mistakes

- Squeezing the mitt too tight, so the glove stabs at the ball.
- Yanking the ball back into the zone, which umpires notice and call a ball.
- Leaving the throwing hand out where a foul tip can hit it. Keep it behind the back or behind the leg.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use tennis balls from closer, catching with a bare hand first to feel a soft catch.
- **Harder:** have a pitcher throw a real bullpen, and have the partner call each pitch a ball or strike.
