For players 13 and up. Pick a ball light enough to throw fast with good form. Only throw when your partner is ready.

## How to run it

1. Stand sideways to your partner in your batting stance, about 12 to 15 feet apart. Hold the ball at your back hip.
2. Load into your back hip, like the start of your swing.
3. Push off the back leg, turn the hips, then the chest, and throw the ball to your partner at chest height.
4. Your partner catches it and throws it back the same way from their stance.
5. Do 3 sets of 6 throws from your hitting side. Then do 1 set from the other side to stay balanced.

## Coaching cues

- "Hips first, then the arms."
- "Throw it through your partner."
- "Finish facing your target."

## Common mistakes

- Throwing with the arms only while the hips stay still.
- Using a ball so heavy that each throw is slow.
- Rounding the back and bending over instead of staying in an athletic stance.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use a lighter ball and do slow throws, focusing on hips before arms.
- **Harder:** add a step-back load before each throw, or throw against a solid block wall by yourself.
