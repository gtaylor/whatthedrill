Start with tennis balls. Wear full gear, and clear the area of bats and buckets before throwing pop-ups near the backstop.

## How to run it

1. The catcher sets up in the crouch behind the plate. The partner stands in front of the plate and throws (or hits with a fungo) a high pop-up behind or beside the plate.
2. The catcher pops up out of the crouch and turns to find the ball. If you wear a two-piece mask, take it off with your throwing hand and hold it until you find the ball.
3. Once you see the ball, toss the mask away from where the ball is coming down so you can't trip on it.
4. Get under the ball with your back to the infield. Foul pop-ups behind the plate drift back toward the field, so set up a step behind where you think it will land.
5. Catch it above your face with two hands. Do 8 to 10, switching between pop-ups behind, to the left and to the right.

## Coaching cues

- "Find it, then toss the mask."
- "Back to the infield."
- "It comes back to you."

## Common mistakes

- Throwing the mask down before finding the ball, then stepping on it.
- Facing the field, so the ball drifts behind you.
- Running to where the ball is now instead of where it will come down.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw lower pop-ups straight up from close by, and skip the mask until the catch is easy.
- **Harder:** use a fungo for higher, spinning pop-ups, add a fence or backstop to work along, or throw two in a row.
