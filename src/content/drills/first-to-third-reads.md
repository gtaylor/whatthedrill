## How to run it

1. Runners line up at first base with helmets on. One coach stands in the outfield with a ball. Another coach stands in the third base coach's box. Put a cone a few steps before second base, out toward the outfield, to start the turn.
2. The runner takes a lead. The outfield coach yells "Hit!" and tosses or rolls the ball to a spot in left, center or right field.
3. The runner sprints, curves out at the cone, and touches the inside corner of second base.
4. If the ball is in front of the runner (left or center field), the runner watches the ball and decides: go to third or hold at second.
5. If the ball is behind the runner (right field), the runner looks to the third base coach for the call around second base.
6. The coach in the outfield throws the ball to third so runners can see whether their choice was right.

## Coaching cues

- "Ball in front, you decide."
- "Ball behind, find your coach."
- "Inside corner, keep going."

## Common mistakes

- Looking back over the shoulder at a ball in right field and slowing down.
- Running straight to second and making a wide, slow turn toward third.
- Stopping on the bag at second before deciding.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** take away the decision. The third base coach always gives the call, and runners just practice the turn and the look.
- **Harder:** add outfielders who field the ball and throw to third, so runners must read how fast the fielder gets to it.
