## How to run it

1. Split into two or more teams of 4 to 6 players. Each team stands in a straight line, players 30 to 40 feet apart (closer for younger players). Mark each spot with a cone.
2. The first player in each line holds a ball. On "Go," they throw to the next player.
3. Each player catches, turns their glove side toward the next player and throws. The ball goes to the end of the line and back.
4. If the ball is dropped or thrown away, the player who missed it gets it, goes back to their cone and throws from there.
5. The first team to get the ball back to the start wins. Play 3 to 5 rounds and change the order each time.

## Coaching cues

- "Turn your glove side."
- "Give a target with two hands."
- "Quick feet, smooth throw."

## Common mistakes

- Rushing and throwing wild. A bad throw costs more time than a slower good one.
- Waiting with the hands down instead of giving a target.
- Catching flat-footed and facing forward, then having to turn before throwing.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** shorten the distance and let players take a step before throwing.
- **Harder:** a throw has to reach the catcher's chest without them moving their feet, or the team does it over.
