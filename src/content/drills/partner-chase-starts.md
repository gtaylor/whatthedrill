## How to run it

1. Put a finish cone 15 to 20 yards away. Partners line up one behind the other, about 2 yards apart, both facing the cone.
2. The front player is the rabbit. The back player is the chaser.
3. Both get in a ready stance, like a base runner's lead stance.
4. The rabbit takes off whenever they want. The chaser goes the moment they see the rabbit move.
5. The chaser tries to tag the rabbit's back before the finish cone. Both run all the way through the cone.
6. Walk back, switch jobs, and go again. Do 6 to 8 rounds.

## Coaching cues

- "React, don't guess."
- "Push the ground back."
- "Run through the cone."

## Common mistakes

- The chaser leaving early before the rabbit moves.
- Popping straight up on the first step instead of driving forward.
- Easing up near the finish because the race is already decided.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** give the rabbit a smaller head start, or make the run shorter.
- **Harder:** start from different positions, like sitting, kneeling, or facing away from the cone.
