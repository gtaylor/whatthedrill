Start with tennis balls or soft balls. Never stare straight at the sun.

## How to run it

1. On a sunny day, set up so the sun is behind the thrower and in the fielder's eyes.
2. The thrower tosses a high fly ball toward the fielder.
3. The fielder raises the glove, open and up, to block the sun. Then they look past the edge of the glove to find the ball.
4. If the ball is still lost, the fielder moves a step or two to the side so the sun isn't right behind it, then catches it to the side of the face.
5. Do 10 reps with soft balls, then switch to baseballs once the fielder catches them cleanly.

## Coaching cues

- "Glove up, block the sun."
- "Look around the glove."
- "Change your angle."

## Common mistakes

- Squinting straight into the sun and hoping to see the ball.
- Holding the glove so low it doesn't shade the eyes.
- Standing still under the ball instead of shifting to a better angle.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use lower, shorter throws, and wear sunglasses or a cap pulled low.
- **Harder:** use a fungo for higher balls, and switch the side the sun is on between reps.
