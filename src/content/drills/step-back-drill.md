## How to run it

1. Set the tee where you normally make contact. Stand with your feet together near where your front foot lands.
2. Step your back foot back into your normal stance.
3. As the back foot lands, sink into your back hip. Feel your weight on the inside of that back leg.
4. From there, stride with your front foot and swing hard through the ball.
5. Hit 3 rounds of 8. Make every step back, load and swing one smooth move.

## Coaching cues

- "Step back and sit in the back hip."
- "Load, then go."
- "Push the ground with your back leg."

## Common mistakes

- Stepping back but staying stiff and tall, so nothing loads.
- Pausing too long after the step, which kills the rhythm.
- Letting weight drift onto the outside of the back foot.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do the step back and load with no ball. Hold the load for a second, then swing.
- **Harder:** do the drill off front toss so you have to time the step to the toss.
