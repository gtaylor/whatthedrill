Use a light band. It should feel like work, never pain. Stop if anything hurts and tell a parent or coach. This adds to, and doesn't replace, following pitch-count and rest guidelines such as Pitch Smart.

## How to run it

1. Tie the band at elbow height to something sturdy, like a fence post or a door anchor.
2. Outward turns: stand sideways to the band with the elbow bent and tucked at your side, a rolled towel between elbow and ribs. Slowly turn the hand away from your belly, then back. 10 to 15 reps.
3. Inward turns: face the other way and slowly turn the hand in toward your belly. 10 to 15 reps.
4. Pull-aparts: hold the band in front of you at shoulder height with both hands. Pull it apart and squeeze your shoulder blades. 10 to 15 reps.
5. Do the routine 2 or 3 times a week, or as part of your warm-up before throwing.

## Coaching cues

- "Slow out, slower back."
- "Elbow stays at your side."
- "Squeeze the shoulder blades."

## Common mistakes

- Using a band that's too strong, so the body swings to finish the rep.
- Going fast and letting the band snap back.
- Letting the elbow slide away from the side during the turns.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do the moves with no band, or step closer to the anchor so the band is looser.
- **Harder:** add a second set, and only move to a slightly stronger band if your form stays perfect.
