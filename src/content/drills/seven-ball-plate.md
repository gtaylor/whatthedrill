The tosser or pitcher stays behind an L-screen. Don't step on the balls on the plate.

## How to run it

1. Mark seven old balls with big numbers, 1 to 7. Line them up across the plate. Balls 2 to 6 cover the plate, and 1 and 7 sit just off each edge.
2. A partner gives front toss or feeds a machine from behind an L-screen.
3. First, just track. Don't swing. Call out the number where each pitch crossed the plate.
4. Next, the coach calls a range, like "3 to 5 only." Swing at pitches in that range and take everything else.
5. Take 3 rounds of 10. Count how many good swings and good takes you make.

## Coaching cues

- "Know your numbers before the pitch."
- "Track it all the way to the plate."
- "If it's not your pitch, take it."

## Common mistakes

- Swinging at balls 1 and 7, which are outside the zone.
- Guessing a number without watching the ball cross the plate.
- Pulling the head off the ball when you decide to take.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use only three balls (inside, middle, outside) and swing at the middle one.
- **Harder:** shrink the range to one or two numbers, or call the range right before each pitch.
