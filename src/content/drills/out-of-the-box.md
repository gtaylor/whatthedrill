## How to run it

1. Set a base 60 feet from home (shorter for younger players) and a cone about 15 feet past it in foul territory.
2. Take a full, dry swing from your batting stance. Let the bat go low and to the side, never behind you.
3. Push off hard and take your first steps straight down the line. Stay low for the first few strides, then run tall.
4. Hit the front edge of the bag at full speed and keep sprinting to the cone. Break down after the cone, then look right for an overthrow.
5. Walk back and rest. Do 6 to 8 runs. A partner can time each one so you can try to beat your best.

## Coaching cues

- "Swing, drop, go."
- "Run through the bag, not to it."
- "Front edge of the base."

## Common mistakes

- Watching the ball after contact instead of running.
- Slowing down, jumping or lunging at the bag, which is slower than running through it.
- Drifting toward fair territory instead of running a straight line.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** skip the swing and start from an athletic stance in the box.
- **Harder:** a partner calls "through" or "turn" after your swing, so you have to decide on the move whether to run through or take a turn.
