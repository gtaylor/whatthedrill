## How to run it

1. Lay an agility ladder flat on grass. No ladder? Draw one with chalk on the driveway, about 10 boxes long.
2. Pattern 1: run through with one foot in each box. Then two feet in each box.
3. Pattern 2: stand sideways and step both feet into each box, then both out, moving down the ladder. Go both directions.
4. Pattern 3: the in-in-out shuffle. Step in with one foot, in with the other, then out to the side, and repeat down the ladder.
5. Do each pattern 2 or 3 times. Walk back to the start between reps.
6. Finish with a 10-yard sprint out of the end of the ladder.

## Coaching cues

- "Quick, light feet."
- "Stay on the balls of your feet."
- "Eyes up, not down."

## Common mistakes

- Going too fast and stepping on the rungs.
- Staring at your feet the whole time.
- Letting the arms hang instead of pumping them.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** walk each pattern slowly first, then speed up once it feels easy.
- **Harder:** add a partner who points left or right at the end, and you sprint that way.
