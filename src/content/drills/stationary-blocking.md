Wear full catcher's gear: helmet with mask, throat guard, chest protector, shin guards and a cup.

## How to run it

1. Start already in the blocking position: on both knees, glove on the ground between the knees, throwing hand behind the glove, chin tucked, shoulders rounded forward.
2. The partner kneels or stands about 15 feet away and throws balls that bounce right in front of the catcher.
3. The catcher keeps still and lets the ball hit the chest protector, so it drops in front. Don't try to catch it.
4. After 10 reps, start from the secondary stance (runners-on stance). On each ball in the dirt, drop both knees to the ground at the same time and get into the blocking position.
5. Do 10 more, then switch or rest.

## Coaching cues

- "Glove fills the hole."
- "Chin down, shoulders forward."
- "Keep it in front."

## Common mistakes

- Trying to catch the ball with the glove instead of blocking it with the body.
- Lifting the chin, which leaves the throat open and the ball flies past.
- Leaning back, so the ball bounces far away instead of dropping close.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use tennis balls or soft safety balls and throw softly.
- **Harder:** throw harder and from a pitching distance, and have the catcher pop up and find the ball after each block.
