Runners run *through* first on a ground ball to the infield. They *take a turn* on a hit to the outfield. This drill is the turn.

## How to run it

1. Players line up at home plate with helmets on. A coach stands in shallow right field holding a ball.
2. On "go," the first runner takes a dry swing and sprints down the line.
3. About 15 feet before first base, the runner curves a few steps out toward foul territory. A cone marks where to start the curve.
4. The runner touches the inside corner of the bag without slowing down, then keeps running hard toward second for 2 or 3 strides.
5. The runner finds the coach. If the coach holds the ball, the runner goes back to first. If the coach drops it, the runner keeps going to second.
6. The next runner goes when the one ahead is clear.

## Coaching cues

- "Start your curve early."
- "Hit the inside corner."
- "Find the ball."

## Common mistakes

- Starting the curve too late and swinging far out into right field.
- Slowing down or stutter-stepping to touch the bag.
- Looking down at the base instead of up at the ball.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** set a line of cones to show the curved path all the way around the bag.
- **Harder:** the coach throws the ball to a fielder at second, and runners decide on their own whether to go.
