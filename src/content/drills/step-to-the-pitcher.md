## How to run it

1. Draw a chalk line, or lay a spare bat on the ground, from the hitter's front foot straight toward the pitcher.
2. Have each hitter take 5 dry swings, stepping so the front foot lands on or next to the line.
3. A coach pitches soft balls or wiffle balls from a short distance. The hitter swings and checks where the front foot landed.
4. Give one point for every stride that lands on the line, whether the ball is hit or not.
5. Rotate hitters every 8 to 10 swings. The players waiting stand well back and count each other's points.

## Coaching cues

- "Land on the line."
- "Step at the pitcher, not the dugout."
- "Front shoulder stays in."

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the front foot toward the dugout side, also called "stepping in the bucket."
- Leaning back on the heels before the pitch arrives.
- Pitching too fast. Keep the speed easy until the step is consistent.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** hit off a tee with the same line before facing any pitches.
- **Harder:** move to regular baseballs at coach-pitch speed once the player has 8 of 10 strides on the line.
