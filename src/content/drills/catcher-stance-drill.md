## How to run it

1. Sign stance: squat with knees pointing forward and the glove resting on the left knee so it hides your signs from the side.
2. Primary stance (no runners, fewer than two strikes): feet about shoulder width, weight on the inside of the feet, glove out in front with a relaxed arm. Hide the throwing hand behind your back or behind your leg.
3. Secondary stance (runners on or two strikes): spread the feet a bit wider and raise the butt so your thighs are close to flat. Keep the throwing hand in a loose fist behind the mitt.
4. Hold each stance for 10 seconds in front of a mirror or camera. Check that your back is flat and you are balanced, not on your heels.
5. Have a partner or parent call "signs," "primary" or "secondary" and move to it. Do 15 to 20 calls.

## Coaching cues

- "Weight forward, not on your heels."
- "Butt up when runners are on."
- "Hide the throwing hand."

## Common mistakes

- Sitting back on the heels, which makes it hard to move or block.
- Holding the throwing hand out where a foul tip can hit it.
- Using the primary stance with runners on base, so you're slow to throw or block.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** hold each stance for only 5 seconds, and rest between calls.
- **Harder:** on each "secondary" call, also pop up into a throwing position or drop into a block.
