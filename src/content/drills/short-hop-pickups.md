## How to run it

1. Partners kneel facing each other, 10 to 15 feet apart, gloves on.
2. Take turns throwing so the ball bounces just in front of your partner's glove.
3. The fielder starts with the glove on the dirt, fingers down, and works it up through the ball to the belly.
4. Do 10 to the forehand, 10 straight on and 10 to the backhand, then switch.
5. Stand up into an athletic stance (feet wide, butt down) and repeat.

## Coaching cues

- "Start the glove on the dirt."
- "See it into the glove."
- "Soft hands. Give with it."

## Common mistakes

- The glove starts high and stabs down at the ball.
- Fielding the ball beside the body instead of out front.
- Turning the head or closing the eyes on the bounce.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use tennis balls, or roll the ball instead of bouncing it. Great for players 8 and under.
- **Harder:** throw harder, mix forehands and backhands at random, and finish every pickup with a quick throw back.
