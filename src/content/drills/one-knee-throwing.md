## How to run it

1. Partners kneel 15 to 20 feet apart (closer for younger players). Throwing-side knee down, glove-side knee up and pointing at your partner.
2. Grip the ball across the seams with a four-seam grip.
3. Take the ball out of the glove and bring the throwing arm up so the elbow is at shoulder height.
4. Point the glove at your partner, then turn your shoulders and throw. Finish with your throwing hand outside your front knee.
5. Make 10 to 15 throws each, then both move back a few steps.

## Coaching cues

- "Point, then throw."
- "Elbow up to your shoulder."
- "Finish by your knee."

## Common mistakes

- Pushing the ball like a dart instead of making a full arm circle.
- Letting the elbow drop below the shoulder.
- Throwing only with the arm and not turning the shoulders.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use a soft or foam ball and move closer together.
- **Harder:** back up a few steps at a time, then stand up and keep the same arm path.
