Wear full catcher's gear, including a helmet with mask, throat guard, chest protector, shin guards and a cup, since balls are thrown at the catcher.

## How to run it

1. The catcher sets up in the secondary stance. A partner kneels or stands about 20 feet away and tosses balls to the catcher's chest.
2. Catch the ball and bring the mitt straight up toward the throwing shoulder. The throwing hand meets it there.
3. Grab the ball, find a four-seam grip if you can, and hold the ball up by your ear in throwing position. Freeze. That is one rep.
4. After 10 good transfers, add the feet: as you catch, step the right foot under you (left foot for lefties), then step the front foot toward the partner and make a short, firm throw.
5. Do 10 to 15 throws. Speed up only when the transfer is clean every time.

## Coaching cues

- "Glove to the shoulder."
- "Hands meet in the middle."
- "Ball to the ear."

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the mitt down to the belly or around the body before taking the ball out.
- Fishing for the ball with the throwing hand and dropping it.
- Rushing the feet and the throw before the transfer is clean.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** drop the throw and do only catch-and-transfer reps, slowly.
- **Harder:** have the partner toss to different spots (high, low, to each side) so the catcher must move the mitt and still transfer fast.
