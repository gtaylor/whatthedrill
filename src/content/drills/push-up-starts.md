## How to run it

1. Put a cone at the start and another cone 10 yards away on flat grass.
2. Lie face down behind the start cone, hands under your shoulders like the bottom of a push-up.
3. On your own "go," or a partner's clap, push the ground away hard and get your feet under you.
4. Stay low and drive your knees forward for the first few steps. Sprint all the way through the far cone.
5. Walk back slowly. That's your rest.
6. Do 5 to 8 starts.

## Coaching cues

- "Push the ground away."
- "Stay low, then rise."
- "Punch your knees forward."

## Common mistakes

- Standing all the way up before taking the first step.
- Taking tiny, choppy steps instead of strong pushes.
- Slowing down before the finish cone.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start from your knees or from a crouch with your hands on the ground.
- **Harder:** start lying on your back, or add a partner to race and see who gets out first.
