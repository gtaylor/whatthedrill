## How to run it

1. Line the players up at home plate, all in helmets. Put a cone in foul territory 10 to 15 feet past first base.
2. One at a time, each player runs from home to first. The goal is to touch the base and keep running to the cone.
3. Tell them to step on the front edge of the bag, not to jump on it.
4. After the cone, they slow down, turn to the right into foul territory, and walk back to the line.
5. Once everyone has it, add a swing: each player hits off the tee, drops the bat (no throwing it), and runs through first.

## Coaching cues

- "Run to the cone, not to the base."
- "Step on the front of the bag."
- "Drop the bat, then go."

## Common mistakes

- Slowing down or stopping right on top of the base.
- Jumping or sliding into first, which is slower and can cause injuries.
- Throwing the bat or watching the ball before starting to run.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** have a coach stand at the cone and give a high five to every runner who makes it there.
- **Harder:** time each runner from home to first and see who can beat their own time.
