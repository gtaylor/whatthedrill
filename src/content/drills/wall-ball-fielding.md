Use a solid brick or concrete wall with no windows nearby, and ask before you use it.

## How to run it

1. Stand about 10 to 15 feet from the wall in your fielding stance: feet wider than your shoulders, knees bent, butt down.
2. Throw a tennis ball low so it bounces once on the ground, hits the wall and rolls or hops back to you.
3. Field it with your glove starting on the ground, out in front of your body, and bring it up to your belly.
4. Do 10 straight at you, then 10 a step to your glove side and 10 a step to your backhand side.
5. When you can catch 10 in a row cleanly, move a couple of steps closer so the ball comes back faster.

## Coaching cues

- "Glove on the ground first."
- "Catch it out front."
- "Stay low the whole time."

## Common mistakes

- Standing up tall between reps and bending only at the waist.
- Starting the glove high and stabbing down at the ball.
- Throwing too hard and turning it into a game of chase.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** stand farther back and roll the ball at the wall so it comes back slow and on the ground.
- **Harder:** move closer, throw harder, or field with a flat paddle glove so you must use two hands.
