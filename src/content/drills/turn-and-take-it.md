Use only tennis balls or foam balls, tossed softly. Never use a real baseball for this drill.

## How to run it

1. The hitter wears a helmet and gets in a normal stance. Walk through the move slowly with no ball first.
2. **The move:** turn the front shoulder in toward the catcher so the back faces the pitcher. Tuck the chin to the chest, and pull the hands and bat in close to the belly.
3. From 15 to 20 feet away, the tosser softly lobs a tennis ball at the hitter's back hip. The hitter turns and lets it bounce off their back.
4. Once that is easy, toss a mix: some strikes the hitter swings at, and some at the body they turn away from.
5. Cheer every good turn. Stop the drill if the player gets upset, and go back to the slow walk-through.

## Coaching cues

- "Turn your back to the pitcher."
- "Chin down, hands in."
- "Let it hit your back. That's what it's for."

## Common mistakes

- Turning toward the pitcher and showing the chest, face and hands.
- Sticking an arm or hand out to block the ball.
- Falling backward out of the box instead of turning in place.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** roll or bounce the ball at the hitter's legs first, then move up to soft lobs.
- **Harder:** toss a little firmer from a little closer, still with tennis balls, and mix in more strikes so the turn has to be quick.
