## How to run it

1. The fielder stands at their position. The partner stands about 30 feet away, acting as the hitter.
2. The partner lifts a ball to start the "pitch." The fielder takes two small walking steps in.
3. As the partner pretends to swing, the fielder does a small hop and lands with feet wide, knees bent and weight on the balls of the feet. This is the prep step.
4. Right after the landing, the partner rolls a ball to the left, the right or straight in.
5. The fielder pushes off right away, gets to the ball and fields it. Do 10, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "Land as the bat hits."
- "Be light on your toes."
- "Your first step goes toward the ball."

## Common mistakes

- Landing late, so the fielder is still in the air when the ball is coming.
- Standing flat-footed with straight legs.
- Taking a false step backward before going to the ball.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** point to a side before rolling so the fielder knows where it's going.
- **Harder:** use a real fungo or live batting practice, and roll or hit farther to each side.
