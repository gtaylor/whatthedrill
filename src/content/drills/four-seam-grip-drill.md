## How to run it

1. Learn the grip first. Find the seam shaped like a horseshoe. Lay your pointer and middle fingers across it, with your thumb under the ball on the bottom seam. Hold it in your fingers, not your palm.
2. Drop the ball into your glove any way it lands.
3. Reach in with your throwing hand. As you pull the ball out, spin it with your fingers until you feel the seams across your fingertips. Don't look.
4. Stop and check the grip. Give yourself a point if it's right.
5. Do 20 reps, then try to do it faster. Finish with 10 throws to a net or a partner using the grip you found.

## Coaching cues

- "Fingers across the horseshoe."
- "Feel it, don't look."
- "Fingertips, not palm."

## Common mistakes

- Peeking into the glove to find the seams.
- Squeezing the ball deep in the palm.
- Taking forever to fix the grip. In a game, if you can't find it fast, throw with what you have.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start with the ball in your hand and close your eyes while you find the grip.
- **Harder:** have someone toss the ball into your glove. Catch it, find the grip and show it in under a second.
