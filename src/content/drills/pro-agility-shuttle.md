## How to run it

1. Set three cones in a straight line, 5 yards apart. Stand at the middle cone facing straight ahead.
2. Get in an athletic stance with your feet wide and knees bent.
3. On "go," turn and sprint 5 yards to the right cone. Touch the line or the cone with your right hand.
4. Turn and sprint 10 yards all the way to the left cone. Touch it with your left hand.
5. Turn and sprint 5 yards back through the middle cone.
6. Rest at least a minute between runs. Do 4 to 6 runs, starting to the left half the time.

## Coaching cues

- "Get low to turn."
- "Plant your outside foot."
- "Run through the finish."

## Common mistakes

- Standing tall at the turn and taking big, slow steps to stop.
- Rounding the turn wide instead of planting and pushing off.
- Easing up before crossing the middle cone at the end.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** shrink the cones to 3 yards apart and jog the first run.
- **Harder:** time each run and try to beat your best, or have a partner say "go" at random.
