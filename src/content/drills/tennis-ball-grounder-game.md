## How to run it

1. Set up a line of fielders on the infield dirt, about 30 feet from the coach. Mark a "gate" for each fielder with two cones a few steps apart.
2. The coach rolls or softly hits a tennis ball to the first fielder. They get in a ready position, field it, and toss it to a bucket player next to the coach.
3. A clean field scores a point for the team. A ball that gets through the gate is fine; the fielder just jogs to the back of the line.
4. After everyone has gone twice, hit the ball a little harder. Keep the score as a team total, not one-on-one.
5. Finish with a round where the team tries to beat its best score.

## Coaching cues

- "Get down before the ball gets there."
- "Glove out front, eyes on the ball."
- "Miss one? Next ball."

## Common mistakes

- Stepping to the side to get out of the way instead of moving in front of the ball.
- Waiting back on the heels instead of creeping in a step.
- Teasing a player who misses. Keep it a team score so nobody is singled out.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** roll every ball and move the coach closer.
- **Harder:** hit the tennis balls with a bat for real hops, then mix in a few soft baseballs.
