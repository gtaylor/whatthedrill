## How to run it

1. The fielder stands in a ready stance facing the partner, who is about 30 feet away holding a ball.
2. The partner points over the fielder's right or left shoulder.
3. The fielder drops the foot on that side straight back, opening the hips toward the ball. Then the other foot crosses over and the fielder runs.
4. After 3 or 4 running steps, the partner throws the ball high over that shoulder for the fielder to run down and catch.
5. Do 5 to each side, then switch.

## Coaching cues

- "Drop the foot, open the gate."
- "Cross over and run."
- "No backpedaling."

## Common mistakes

- Backpedaling instead of turning, which is slow and can make you trip.
- Dropping the foot sideways instead of back, so the angle is too flat.
- Running with the glove held up the whole way, which slows you down.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do the footwork with no ball until it feels natural, then add short, easy throws.
- **Harder:** point one way, then throw the other way so the fielder has to turn and reroute.
