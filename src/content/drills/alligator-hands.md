## How to run it

1. The fielder gets in a low stance with the glove open on the ground out in front. The glove is the alligator's bottom jaw.
2. The throwing hand floats just above the glove, palm down. That is the top jaw.
3. The partner stands about 15 feet away and rolls the ball slowly right into the glove.
4. As the ball hits the glove, the top hand "bites" down on it. Then both hands pull the ball up to the belly.
5. Do 10 reps, then switch. Speed up the rolls as the fielder gets it.

## Coaching cues

- "Bottom jaw on the ground."
- "Bite it with your top hand."
- "Bring it to your belly."

## Common mistakes

- The top hand slaps down too early and blocks the ball from getting in the glove.
- The glove sits on its side instead of open and facing the ball.
- Catching with one hand and letting the throwing hand hang by the side.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** take the glove off and catch the rolls with two bare hands first.
- **Harder:** roll the ball a step to each side so the fielder has to move, then bite it.
