Teach the calling rules before any balls go up, and start with soft throws so no one runs into each other.

## How to run it

1. Put three outfielders in left, center and right, a bit closer together than in a game. Extra players wait in line behind each spot.
2. Teach the rules: call "Ball! Ball! Ball!" loud and early. Center fielder has priority. When you hear someone else call it, yell "Take it!" and peel off to back them up.
3. The coach throws or hits fly balls into the gaps between two fielders.
4. Both fielders go after it. One calls it, catches it and throws it in. The other backs up behind them.
5. After each ball, the fielders rotate to the next line so everyone plays every spot.

## Coaching cues

- "Call it three times, loud."
- "Center field wins."
- "Hear a call? Back them up."

## Common mistakes

- Calling it once, too quietly, or too late.
- Both players calling it and neither giving way.
- Stopping and watching instead of backing up the player who called it.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw by hand, and say the name of the player who should call it before you throw.
- **Harder:** add the shortstop and second baseman so outfielders practice calling off infielders on shallow flies.
