Lay the board flat on firm, level ground. Never stand on it while it's on its edge.

## How to run it

1. Lay the board flat, pointing at the net like the line from home plate to the pitcher.
2. Stand on the board in your batting stance, both feet on it. Set the tee beside the board where you make contact.
3. Take 5 slow dry swings. Stride straight along the board and finish on balance.
4. Now hit balls off the tee. If your front foot steps off the board, you stepped in the bucket.
5. Hit 3 rounds of 10. Hold your finish for a count of two after each swing.

## Coaching cues

- "Stride at the pitcher."
- "Front toe stays on the board."
- "Hold your finish."

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the front foot off toward the dugout as you swing.
- Leaning way forward to stay on the board instead of staying tall.
- Rushing swings so you never check your balance.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** put a bat or a strip of tape on the ground instead of a board, and stride along it.
- **Harder:** stay on the board while a partner gives you front toss from behind a screen.
