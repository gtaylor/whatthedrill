## How to run it

1. Partners stand 30 to 40 feet apart. The pitcher starts in the stride position: feet as wide as a normal stride, front foot pointed at the partner, body sideways.
2. Reach the glove arm out toward your partner at about shoulder height.
3. Bring the throwing arm up and back with the elbow about level with the shoulder.
4. Throw. As the arm comes forward, keep the glove out in front and bring your chest over to it. The glove ends up near your chest, not yanked behind your hip.
5. Make 10 to 15 throws, then try the same feel from a full pitching motion.

## Coaching cues

- "Chest to the glove."
- "Glove stays in front."
- "Point, turn, throw."

## Common mistakes

- Pulling the glove hard behind the back, which spins the shoulders open early.
- Landing the front foot open, so the toes point away from the target.
- Standing straight up at the finish instead of getting the chest out over the front leg.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do it with no ball in front of a mirror so you can see the glove.
- **Harder:** throw from a full motion to a catcher, who checks whether the glove stayed in front.
