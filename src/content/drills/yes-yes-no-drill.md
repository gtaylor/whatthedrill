The tosser stays behind an L-screen for every pitch.

## How to run it

1. A partner gives front toss from behind an L-screen. Mix in strikes and some balls on purpose.
2. As the tosser starts the toss, the hitter starts the load and says "yes."
3. Keep saying "yes" as the ball comes in. Plan to swing at every pitch.
4. If the pitch is a ball, say "no" and hold the swing. If it's a strike, swing hard.
5. Take 3 rounds of 10. The tosser keeps count of good swings and good takes.

## Coaching cues

- "Get ready early."
- "Yes, yes, yes, then hit it."
- "No means hold."

## Common mistakes

- Starting the load late, then rushing the swing.
- Waiting to decide to swing instead of planning to swing every pitch.
- Saying "no" but swinging anyway at pitches in the dirt or over the head.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** throw only strikes at first so the hitter just practices getting ready early.
- **Harder:** use a pitching machine at game speed and add more balls just off the plate.
