## How to run it

1. The pitcher and a partner stand about 35 to 45 feet apart, a little shorter than full pitching distance. The pitcher stands sideways with the glove shoulder pointing at the partner.
2. Lift the front knee to about belt height, with the foot hanging under the knee. Keep the hands together at the chest and the head over the back foot.
3. Hold that position for a count of two. If you wobble, reset and try again.
4. From the hold, stride straight at your partner. Keep the front shoulder pointed at the target until the front foot lands, then throw.
5. Make 10 to 15 throws. The partner checks that the front foot landed in line with them.

## Coaching cues

- "Tall and still."
- "Shoulder stays on the target."
- "Stride straight, then throw."

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back toward second base at the top of the leg lift.
- Lifting the knee so high that balance is lost.
- Opening the front shoulder and glove early as the stride starts.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** practice the lift and hold with no ball in front of a mirror.
- **Harder:** shorten the hold to a quick pause, then throw to a catcher's target from full distance.
