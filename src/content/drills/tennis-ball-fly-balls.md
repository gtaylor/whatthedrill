## How to run it

1. Stand about 15 feet apart. The fielder holds the glove up by the face, ready.
2. Toss a tennis ball high and straight to the fielder.
3. The fielder keeps their eyes on the ball, moves under it, and catches it above the head on the glove side, fingers pointing up.
4. Squeeze the glove and cover the ball with the throwing hand.
5. After 10 good catches, toss them a few steps in front, behind or to the side so the fielder has to move to get under them.

## Coaching cues

- "Eyes on the ball the whole way."
- "Catch it above your head."
- "Two hands. Cover it up."

## Common mistakes

- Holding the glove down by the waist and catching the ball like a basket.
- Running to the spot with eyes down, then looking up too late.
- Turning the head away or closing the eyes as the ball comes down.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** catch without a glove using two hands, or toss lower and closer.
- **Harder:** toss higher and farther, or hit the tennis ball high with a tennis racket.
