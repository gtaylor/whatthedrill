Hitters and runners wear helmets. The coach pitches from behind an L-screen in front of the mound.

## How to run it

1. Put a full defense in the field. Split the rest into hitters and base runners.
2. Start with a runner on first. The hitter's job is a sacrifice bunt toward first base to move the runner to second.
3. The coach pitches from behind an L-screen. The defense plays it live and makes the throw.
4. Next, put runners on first and second. The hitter bunts toward third base.
5. Each hitter gets 2 bunts, then rotates to runner, then to the field. Score 1 point for every runner moved up.

## Coaching cues

- "Get the bunt down first."
- "Pick your side before the pitch."
- "Bunt strikes, take balls."

## Common mistakes

- Bunting the ball hard back to the pitcher.
- Squaring around so late that there's no time to get set.
- Running before the ball is on the ground.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** take away the defense and just have runners go on the bunt.
- **Harder:** add the squeeze play with a runner on third, or let the defense call a bunt play.
