## How to run it

1. Jog easily for 2 to 3 minutes, like out to the outfield and back, to get warm.
2. Arm circles: start small and make them bigger. 10 forward, then 10 backward.
3. Arm hugs: swing both arms across the chest like a hug, then open them wide. 10 times.
4. Walking lunges with a twist toward the front leg, then trunk turns side to side. 10 each.
5. Finish with 10 shoulder blade squeezes, then start the team throwing routine.

## Coaching cues

- "Start small, get bigger."
- "Keep moving, don't hold."
- "Smooth, not fast."

## Common mistakes

- Skipping the warm-up and going straight to throwing.
- Whipping the arms as fast as possible instead of moving under control.
- Holding long, still stretches before throwing instead of moving stretches.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do fewer reps and follow a coach who calls out each move.
- **Harder:** add high knees, butt kicks and side shuffles to the jog.
