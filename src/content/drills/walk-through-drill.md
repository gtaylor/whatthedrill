## How to run it

1. Set the tee where you normally make contact. Start about two steps behind your normal stance, toward the catcher.
2. Step forward with your front foot. Then cross your back foot behind it.
3. As the back foot lands, load into the back hip and stride.
4. Swing hard and let your momentum carry you into a balanced finish.
5. Hit 3 rounds of 6. Rest between rounds so every swing is at full effort.

## Coaching cues

- "Walk, load, swing."
- "Keep moving. Don't stop and start."
- "Swing like you mean it."

## Common mistakes

- Lunging the head and chest forward past the front foot.
- Walking in a curve so you end up too far from or too close to the tee.
- Stopping dead after the walk, which throws away the momentum.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do slow walk-throughs with no ball until the steps feel natural.
- **Harder:** do it off front toss, starting your walk as the tosser starts the toss.
