## How to run it

1. Set three balls on the ground about a big step in front of you: one straight ahead, one to the left and one to the right.
2. Get in your secondary stance (runners-on stance).
3. Block the middle ball: drop both knees at once, glove on the ground over the ball, chin down, shoulders forward.
4. Get back in your stance and block the ball to one side. Step out with the foot on that side, drop the knees, and angle your shoulders back toward home plate so the ball would bounce toward the middle.
5. Return to your stance and block the other side. Do 5 rounds of all three.

## Coaching cues

- "Get there, then drop."
- "Turn your shoulders to the plate."
- "Glove covers the ball."

## Common mistakes

- Reaching with the glove instead of moving the body in front of the ball.
- Squaring up straight on side blocks, so the ball would kick off to the side.
- Getting up slowly between reps, which makes it too easy.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** do the side blocks at walking speed and just hold the position for 2 seconds.
- **Harder:** have a partner call out "left," "middle" or "right" in a random order, as fast as you can recover.
