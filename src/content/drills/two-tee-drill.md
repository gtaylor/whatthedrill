## How to run it

1. Set up the hitting tee where you normally make contact, with a ball on it.
2. Put a second tee with no ball about a foot behind the first one, toward the catcher. Set it a couple of inches lower.
3. Take slow swings and hit the ball without touching the back tee.
4. If you clip the back tee, your hands dropped and the barrel dipped too early. Reset and try again.
5. Build up to full-speed swings. Hit 3 rounds of 10.

## Coaching cues

- "Hands stay on top."
- "Barrel above the back tee."
- "Short to the ball."

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the back shoulder to lift the ball, which makes the barrel dip.
- Moving the back tee farther away after every miss instead of fixing the swing.
- Swinging softly forever. Speed up once the path is clean.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** move the back tee farther back and lower, then bring it closer as you get cleaner.
- **Harder:** raise the hitting tee to the top of the zone, where dropping the hands causes pop-ups.
