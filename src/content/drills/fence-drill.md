Use a chain-link fence or a hitting net. Never a wall, a window or a car.

## How to run it

1. Face the fence. Put the knob of the bat on your belly button and touch the end of the bat to the fence. That's your distance.
2. Without moving your feet, turn into your batting stance so the fence is where home plate would be.
3. Take slow swings. The bat should not touch the fence.
4. Speed up a little at a time until you're swinging at full speed without hitting it.
5. When that's easy, move a couple of inches closer.

## Coaching cues

- "Knob to the ball."
- "Keep your hands close to your body."
- "Turn, don't push."

## Common mistakes

- Sneaking a step away from the fence before each swing.
- Dropping the back shoulder to make room, which leads to an uppercut.
- Staying slow forever. The goal is a short swing at full speed.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** start a fist's width farther away than one bat-length.
- **Harder:** move closer, then go straight to a tee and take 5 swings with the same feel.
