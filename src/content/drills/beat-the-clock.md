Wear full catcher's gear: helmet with mask, throat guard, chest protector, shin guards and a cup.

## How to run it

1. Put the catcher behind home plate in the secondary stance. A middle infielder covers second base. The coach or a pitcher throws from the mound or a bit closer.
2. A second coach stands to the side with a stopwatch.
3. On each pitch, the catcher catches, transfers and throws to second. The infielder catches it at the bag and makes a tag.
4. Start the watch when the ball hits the catcher's mitt. Stop it when it hits the infielder's glove. That is the "pop time."
5. Say the time out loud after each throw. Do sets of 5 throws, rest, and try to beat your best time while still throwing accurately.

## Coaching cues

- "Quick feet, short steps."
- "Throw through the bag."
- "Accurate beats fast."

## Common mistakes

- Taking long, slow steps out of the crouch.
- Rushing so much that the throw sails or bounces far off the bag.
- Throwing too many in a row. Tired arms throw slow and wild.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** move second base closer, or throw to a player at the edge of the infield grass.
- **Harder:** add a batter standing in (no swing) and pitches in the dirt or off to the side.
