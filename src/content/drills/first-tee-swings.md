Only the hitter stands near the tee. Everyone else waits well back, behind a line or a cone.

## How to run it

1. **Grip.** Hands together near the knob. The glove hand goes on the bottom. Hold the bat in the fingers, not deep in the palm.
2. **Stance.** Feet a little wider than the shoulders, knees bent, toes pointing at the plate. Hands up near the back shoulder.
3. **Distance.** Stand so the end of the bat reaches just past the far side of the ball. Set the tee so the ball sits even with the front foot.
4. **Swing.** Keep the eyes on the ball, take a small step toward the pitcher, and swing through the ball.
5. **Finish.** The back foot turns so the shoelaces face the pitcher, and the bat ends up over the front shoulder.
6. Hit 10 balls, reset the stance before each one, then take a short break.

## Coaching cues

- "Glove hand on the bottom."
- "Eyes on the ball until it's gone."
- "Squish the bug with your back foot."

## Common mistakes

- Holding the hands apart or with the wrong hand on the bottom.
- Standing so far away that the hitter has to reach and lean.
- Looking up to see where the ball went before the bat hits it.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** use a big ball and a light bat, and just work on hitting the ball off the tee.
- **Harder:** put a target out in front, like a bucket or a cone, and try to hit line drives at it.
