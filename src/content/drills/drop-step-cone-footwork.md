## How to run it

1. Put one cone where you stand. Put the other two about 10 steps behind you, one back to the left and one back to the right, like a V.
2. Start in your outfield ready stance, facing forward, weight on the balls of your feet.
3. Pick a side. Open up by stepping back with the foot on that side (that's the drop step), then cross the other leg over and sprint to the cone.
4. While you run, look back over your shoulder like you're tracking a ball. Keep your head and eyes steady.
5. Jog back and go to the other side. Do 6 to 8 to each side.

## Coaching cues

- "Open the gate."
- "Drop, cross, go."
- "Run on your toes, eyes over the shoulder."

## Common mistakes

- Backpedaling instead of turning and running.
- A first step that goes sideways instead of back at an angle.
- Bouncing your head while you run, which makes a real ball hard to track.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** walk through the drop step and crossover slowly a few times before running.
- **Harder:** have someone point left or right at the last second, or set a phone timer to beep randomly and go on the beep.
